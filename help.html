First visit

Extract your build and open Start Office Studio for your system. Choose a name, logo, accent and practice. Select Create office to meet the crew.

Before you create an office, the setup preview includes its dedicated LEGAL desk and final desk count. LEGAL prepares review questions and checklists for qualified counsel; it does not replace legal advice. The ten-desk limit includes LEGAL. If an imported pack already has ten desks without LEGAL, remove one desk from the JSON and import it again, or choose a different template. The app never removes a desk to make room, and existing offices are not migrated.

Your company name appears in the tower when the office opens. Choose Desks to move inside or Market Room to see the boardroom. Use Desks & drafts above the room for saved work. Choose Task board to see the crew's simulated tasks.

How do I change a desk?

Select it on the floor. In Desk settings, save a display title and color, or reset them to the practice defaults. The Head Executive starts with the company accent and uploaded logo. Choose Add agent in the embedded room to save another crew member and see their desk in the office.

Drafts and providers

The ampersand sculpture uses your office color. It lights up red when the Head Executive needs your attention. The color returns when that alert clears. Select the ampersand in the Studio header to open your saved approval inbox. With reduced motion enabled, the alert stays visible without pulsing.

Pick a desk, write a sample brief, and select Prepare draft. Open the result in the approval inbox. Approve or decline it to record your decision. Room animations and the simulated room task board are separate from the saved inbox.

How do I connect a model?

Open Configure Providers for your operating system. Choose OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or Meta Muse. Enter a model ID available to your API account, then type the key at the hidden terminal prompt. Press Enter at the provider menu to launch. In the app, select the desk and save its preferred model.

What if a provider is unavailable?

Choose Local demo · this draft only to try the workflow without credits. For live work, check the key and model ID on the computer running the server. The first successful live draft confirms access. Account keys stay on that computer.

Your company's manual

Under the workspace rules, save the HTTPS link to your team's operating manual. Each office has its own link. Open it from the workspace header or from The Field in a compatible room. Saving the link does not import its pages or attach them to model requests.

To use a workflow in a draft, include the relevant approved instructions in the brief. A live provider receives that brief, the desk instructions and the workspace rules. Use fictional inputs for a demonstration.

Saving and handoff

Offices, branding and decisions stay on the host computer. Use the office selector to switch businesses. The ZIP transfers the application. A colleague opening it on another computer starts with their own offices and provider setup.
